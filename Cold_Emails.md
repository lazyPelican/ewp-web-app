# Cold Email Outreach — Quote App

Each email is short, written to sound like a real person, and personalized per industry. Mix and match. Don't send all of them in a batch — space them out, ideally Tue–Thu mornings (8–10am Central), and follow up once after 4–5 days.

**Before sending each one, edit the bracketed personalization line.** That single sentence is the difference between "another freelancer email" and "this person actually looked at our shop."

---

## Subject lines (rotate — don't reuse the same one across the whole list)

1. `Quick one for [Company]`
2. `Saw your work — quote app idea`
3. `[First name] — quoting question`
4. `Built this for a shop near you`
5. `Stealing your afternoon back`

Avoid `Increase your sales by 50%!` style subjects. Lowercase first letters and a typo-free casual tone read as human, not marketing.

# 🧠 SMALL THINGS THAT MAKE COLD EMAILS WORK

- **Send from a real domain**, not Gmail. `you@yourstudio.com` lands in inbox; `randomname123@gmail.com` lands in spam.
- **No tracking pixels, no marketing footer**, no unsubscribe legalese — those scream "blast" and tank delivery.
- **One link max** in the body. Each link is a spam-score increment.
- **Plain text > HTML**. Send these as plain-text emails, not formatted newsletters.
- **Don't attach the deck** to the cold email. Attachments to strangers go to spam. Send the deck only after they reply.
- **Personalize the first line**. The bracketed `[the patio work in your gallery]` line is the most important sentence in the entire email — that's what makes it stop reading like a template. Spend 60 seconds on each company's site to write that one line.
- **First-name greeting** when the contact page gives a name. If not, `Hi there` is fine — `Dear Sir/Madam` is dead on arrival.
- **Don't follow up more than twice**. After two emails with no response, move on. They saw it.

# 📋 HOW TO USE THE DECK

- **Don't attach the deck to the cold email.** Save it for after they reply.
- When they reply asking to see more: send the deck **plus** a 5-minute Loom video walking through the actual app. The video is what closes — the deck is the leave-behind they can forward to a co-owner.
- Replace the placeholder `[ Your Name ]`, email, phone, and calendar link on slide 11 before sending. Use [cal.com](https://cal.com) (free) for the calendar link if you don't have one.
